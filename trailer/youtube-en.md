## Description

Who owns your training data? Many fitness apps send your health data to someone else's servers. Alien Fitness stays on your device: no account, no server, no telemetry. The app does not even request an internet permission.

Alien Fitness is a minimal, fully offline workout tracker for Android and GrapheneOS, open source (MIT). Build your own training plans from all exercises or use the built-in ones: strength (Push, Pull, Legs and Core) and HIT (Tabata, Power). Free workouts without a plan, add exercises on the fly.

Live logging set by set with an automatic rest timer. The set field shows what you did last time and pre-fills it. HIT intervals run with a work timer, the rest follows and the next set starts on its own. Progress charts per exercise for max weight or reps. Exercise library with images, muscle groups and equipment. Training history with a note per session.

All data stays in the app on your device. Save and migrate via JSON backup, on import merge or replace everything. An interrupted workout survives an app restart and can be resumed.
